# Snowed-in cabin

Part of the environments engine: [`docs/environments.md`](../../environments.md) has the design and says where each part is.

`app/environments/snowed_in_cabin.env.json` (category `retreat`, author `Gemini 4 Neon Extra High`). *A steep alpine timber A-frame chalet snowed in on a moonlit winter night: roaring river-stone hearth, woven Nordic rug, steaming cocoa, sleeping loft, and snow-laden spruces outside.*

- **The A-frame chalet** (`cabin_chalet`, `scripts/env/systems/kit/snowed_in_cabin.gd`): a steep 45-degree alpine timber A-frame (`half_width = 5.80 m`, `front_z = -8.20 m`, `back_z = +5.60 m`, knee walls `y = 1.15 m`, soaring central ridge beam at `y = 6.95 m`) lined with warm tongue-and-groove timber slopes, longitudinal purlins, and wide honey-oak plank floorboards. Five heavy chamfered Douglas-fir scissor trusses (`z = -7.95, -4.95, -0.55, +2.55, +5.40 m`) with king posts, diagonal scissor struts, knee braces, and sleek gunmetal forged-iron gusset straps & hex bolts carry the roof while leaving a generous `4.40 m` central living bay for the hearth and port dormer window. Beneath both sofas and the coffee table lies a flat hand-loomed woven wool `nordic_rug` (`Procedural.apply(..., "nordic_rug", ...)`, oatmeal cream with crimson, spruce-pine, and ochre diamond bands, spruce border, and cream wool fringe).
- **Monumental snow-rimmed gable windows & screen wall:** the cinema screen (`4.80 × 2.70 m` at `[0.0, 2.35, -7.40]`) is framed between twin floor-to-rafter Douglas-fir gable posts with a chamfered timber border, protective top hood (concealing a subtle downward `PieceCove` strip), matte-charcoal backing slab, and a low built-in timber window-bench credenza below. Left, right, and soaring into the triangular gable apex above the screen, dark timber-mullioned winter panes (`PieceGlass`, non-overlapping around the picture) with sculpted white snow pillows piled along every exterior sill and transom look straight out into a moonlit alpine valley, complemented by a frost-rimmed port dormer window (`z = -4.55..-2.35 m`).
- **Warm alpine interior craft:**
  - **Wagon-wheel beam chandelier:** hung cleanly at `(0.0, 4.24, -2.75)` high above every seat's sight line — a dark chamfered oak wheel rim bound in forged iron with eight spokes, four hanger chains, and eight glowing frosted-amber candle lanterns (`PieceLamps`), paired with indirect `PieceCove` uplight washes tucked along both upper timber purlins.
  - **Low live-edge cedar coffee table** (`(0.0, 0.0, -1.48)`, top `y = 0.40 m <= 0.44 m` — zero sight-line obstruction from any seat): thick live-edge red cedar slab with dark bark edges on splayed forged-iron legs, holding two glazed ceramic hot-cocoa mugs (cream & spruce-green) with faint rising steam wisps, a small glowing brass candle lantern, three sculpted pinecones on a cedar coaster, and a softly folded heritage `tartan_blanket` wool throw draped over the table lip with cream fringe.
  - **Aft sleeping-loft mezzanine & chalet entry:** raised timber sleeping loft (`z = +3.05..+5.60 m`, `y = 2.65 m`) with heavy fascia beam, knee-braced posts, wooden balustrade, slanted eight-rung attic ladder, loft bed with crimson-and-cream alpine quilt, pillows, and warm bedside lantern; below it stands the heavy Z-braced plank chalet entry door with forged-iron strap hinges, ring latch, frost-lit upper window pane, braided doormat, timber coat rack with draped winter scarf, and a leaning pair of classic bent-tip hickory alpine skis & bamboo ski poles with snow baskets. Built-in low honey-pine bookcases packed with jewel-toned winter hardcovers and a brass reading lamp line the port and starboard aft knee walls.
- **Hero craft piece — river-stone hearth** (`cabin_hearth`): built proudly into the starboard living bay (`face x = +3.85 m`, `z = -2.75 m`) over a stepped slate-stone hearth apron (`y = 0.0..0.18 m`, extending aft beneath the firewood cubby):
  - Hand-laid sculpted rounded river-stone boulder courses on both front face and side cheeks, dressed stone arch voussoirs & keystone framing the arched soot-blackened firebox (`y = 0.18..1.24 m`), and a tapered stone chimney stack rising through the 45-degree timber roof with a sleek gunmetal flashing collar.
  - Rough-hewn cedar mantel beam (`y = 1.68 m`) on timber corbels dressed with a double-swagged evergreen spruce & pinecone garland with tiny glowing berry lights, a warm brass mantel lantern, brass candlestick, and warm stoneware jug.
  - Forged-iron grate & brass-finial andirons holding five charred split-oak logs over glowing ember coals and 14 crossed-quad animated dancing fire tongues (`HearthGlow`, `shaders/env_screening_glow.gdshader`, `env_see_through`, slow sub-3 Hz flicker `[0.37, 0.79, 1.27, 2.11] Hz`) plus a soft warm hearth glow cone (`HearthShaft`, `shaders/env_world_shaft.gdshader`, `env_see_through`, shared with the cocoa & kettle steam wisps) and two dynamic stand-in firelight lamps warming the Nordic rug.
  - Wrought-iron folding spark fire screen, brass fireside poker/tongs/shovel tool stand, burnished steaming copper kettle on a cast-iron trivet, and a recessed timber-framed firewood alcove packed with 26 cut split-oak logs.
- **Moonlit alpine winter valley & outside-only snowfall:** `AlpineOutside` (`shaders/env_ice_outside.gdshader`, opaque 1 draw) wraps cleanly outside the chalet footprint with moonlit glittering snowdrifts banked against the outer walls, 59 sculpted snow-laden evergreen spruces framing the forward gable and port dormer windows, a tiny distant glowing A-frame cabin down in the valley, and two layered jagged moonlit alpine mountain ranges ringing the distant horizon beneath a bright winter Moon (`moon_bearing: 354°`, `moon_altitude: 25°`) and starry sky. Exterior GPU snow particles (`weather`, `falls: "snow"`, `900` flakes across two boxes outside the forward gable and port dormer) use gentle outward wind (`[-0.25, -0.08]`) so every flake and its entire drift envelope stays strictly outside the cabin.
- **Seats:** `domestic` (`shell: "kit"`, `half_width: 5.8`, `front_z: -8.2`, `back_z: 5.6`, `height: 5.4`) lays out two rows of three (`6` seats, `seat_pitch: 0.80`, `row_pitch: 1.85`, zero centre aisle) rendered by `seats` (`model: "settee"`, warm cognac `brown_leather` sofas on dark oak feet, dressed with plush crimson & spruce wool corner pillows) so there is always a dead-centre head-on seat at `(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)` facing `-Z` and an odd seat count with a dead-centre seat (`x = 0.0`) in every row.
- **Systems:** `light` (warm timber fill, `baked_fill: 0.28`, `spill: 0.65`, `accent` bound to `setting.hearth`), `domestic`, `seats`, `kit` (`cabin_chalet`, `cabin_hearth`, `bake_lamps: 8`, `flicker: 0.55`, `cove_floor: 0.34`, `water_motion: 0.85`), `sky` (`latitude: 46.5`, `day_of_year: 18`, `hour: 22.8`, `facing: 0`, `dusk: 0.96`, bright Moon & winter stars, clouds bound to `setting.snowfall`), `weather` (outside-only snow bound to `setting.snowfall`), `acoustics` (`small_room`, `rt60: 0.52`, `damping: 0.62`, `wet: 0.20`), `sound_bed` (`fire` at the starboard hearth following `setting.hearth`, muffled outside `wind` following `setting.snowfall`, and a distant `tawny_owl` every `140 s`).
- **Settings:**
  - `snowfall` (*Snowfall*): `blizzard` (*Alpine blizzard*), `gentle` (*Gentle flurries*, default), `clear` (*Clear moonlit night*) — controls outside snow particles, sky snow-cloud cover, and muffled alpine wind.
  - `hearth` (*Hearth fire*): `roaring` (*Roaring fire*, default), `low` (*Low flames*), `embers` (*Glowing embers*) — scales flames, ember coals, hearth glow cone, dynamic stand-in firelight, and fire crackle sound bed smoothly.
  - `cabin_glow` (*Cabin glow*): `amber` (*Warm amber*, default), `frost_blue` (*Frost moonlight*), `dim` (*Dim hearthlight*) — shifts the indirect `PieceCove` purlin and screen-hood washes.
- **Cues:** `film_dim` eases `light.house_lamps` down to `0.18` over `5.0 s` on `starting`, `playing`, and `credits` (keeping `cove_floor: 0.34` and cozy hearth firelight alive); `pause_glow` lifts lamps gently to `0.55` over `2.5 s` on `paused` and `intermission`; `lights_up` returns lamps to `1.0` over `4.0 s` on `preshow` and `ended`.
- **Comfort:** every fire flicker frequency is strictly under `3 Hz` (`0.37`–`2.11 Hz`) and freezes smoothly when `Flickering lights` is off; `Motion` scales flame lick and steam/hearth-glow wisps (`0` freezes drift cleanly); sight lines to the cinema screen are 100 % clear from all six seats; every kit physics box keeps `>= 0.5 m` clearance from `(0, 0, 0)`; every snowfall emitter and its wind-drift envelope stays strictly outside the cabin.

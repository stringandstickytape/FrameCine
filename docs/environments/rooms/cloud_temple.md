# Cloud temple

Part of the environments engine: [`docs/environments.md`](../../environments.md) has the design and says where each part is.

`app/environments/cloud_temple.env.json` (category `world`, author `Gemini 4 Neon Extra High`). *An open-air white-marble sanctuary suspended above an endless golden-dusk sea of clouds: sixteen fluted columns, a sky-reflecting water basin, four bronze braziers, and rising stars.*

- **The floating sanctuary** (`cloud_sanctuary`, `scripts/env/systems/kit/cloud_temple.gd`): a circular stepped white-marble and warm travertine crepidoma platform (`r = 12.6 m`, stylobate deck at `y = -0.24 m`, underside carved stepped corbel down to `y = -2.18 m`) hovering serenely above the cloud abyss. At the heart of the stylobate (`(0, -1.5)`), a circular `mosaic_medallion` tesserae plaza (`r = 4.2 m`) is framed concentrically by inlaid reflective brass astrolabe floor rings (`r = 4.2`, `6.65`, `9.30 m`) and seven radial compass spokes (trimmed cleanly clear of the water basin).
- **The fluted colonnade & open entablature:** sixteen tall classical white-marble columns (`h = 6.80 m`, `r = 0.40 m`, sixteen flutes with subtle entasis split into two non-overlapping 180-degree lightmap sheets, square plinth, Attic double-torus base, gilt astragal necking collar, carved Ionic bolster scrolls and square abacus) ring the platform on two sweeping arcs (`±27°` to `±168°`), framing the open screen portal ahead and the astrolabe portal behind. Each arc carries a broken classical entablature (marble architrave with gilt taenia moulding, warm travertine triglyph frieze with gilt diamond metope rosettes, dentil cornice and gilt palmette acroteria crowning the end columns) left completely open overhead to the golden twilight sky and the rising Milky Way.
- **Architectural cove light (`PieceCove`):** warm golden architectural light glows strictly from recessed stone channels — the underside soffit of both curved classical architraves, recessed Attic plinth collars, the inner screen jamb reveals and cornice soffit, the inner marble rim of the reflecting basin, and the recessed frieze beneath the aft altar tabletop — keeping `cove_floor: 0.35` so the sanctuary stays gently legible during film playback while every fluted marble shaft remains crisp from base to capital.
- **Reflecting basin & screen pylons:** directly beneath and in front of the cinema screen (`6.8 × 3.82 m` at `[0.0, 3.05, -11.4]`), a shallow marble-coped water basin (`z = -10.88` to `-6.28 m`, `x = -4.25` to `+4.25 m`, `shaders/env_world_water.gdshader`, opaque 1 draw) shifts from deep twilight-indigo water catching golden sunset caustics and specular shimmer to midnight-sapphire starlight water reflecting the film's spill. Twin monumental fluted white-marble screen pylons (`x = ±4.06 m`, `z = -11.62 m`) with gilt capitals, urn finials, a stepped cornice and a gilded sunburst acroterion frame the screen over a dark slate backing slab so bright sunset sky never bleeds behind the picture.
- **Bronze tripod braziers & aft brass astrolabe:** four cast-bronze tripod braziers on stepped marble plinths stand cleanly around the outer colonnade perimeter (`(±5.65, -6.25)` and `(±6.35, +2.65)`, well clear of every seat's sight line), with curved bronze legs, gilt knee bosses, fluted fire bowls, glowing ember crowns (`accent` lamp group), and crossed-quad amber flames (`shaders/env_screening_glow.gdshader`, `env_see_through`, slow sub-3 Hz flicker `[0.37, 0.83, 1.31, 2.07] Hz`). Behind the seating (`z = +7.05 m`), a stepped octagonal marble altar with a fluted marble drum supports a gleaming cast-brass armillary astrolabe sphere (horizon, meridian, equatorial and zodiac ecliptic rings pierced by a tilted celestial axis spear around a softly glowing sun orb).
- **The cloud sea, floating sky-islands & sunset god-rays** (`cloud_sky_sea`, `shaders/env_cloud_sea.gdshader` + `shaders/env_world_shaft.gdshader`):
  - `CloudSea` (opaque, 1 draw, `r = 185 m`): a 360-degree disc curving gently from `y = -11.5 m` beneath the sanctuary up to `+2.2 m` at the horizon rim so it merges seamlessly into `SkyDome` using an exact match to `sky_base()`. Four-octave domain-warped billowing cumulus tops catch warm sunset apricot-gold rim light toward `glow_bearing` (`288°`) over indigo-violet leeward troughs, drift glacially with `Motion`, catch subtle film spill on nearby billows, and transition smoothly into silver-blue starlit clouds as `sky.dusk` advances from `0.18` to `0.92`.
  - `SkyIslands` (opaque, 1 draw): four distant craggy limestone floating islands (`66` to `96 m` out) plunging into cloud-sea mist and crowned with sunlit broken marble triumphal arches, ruined colonnades, a circular monopteros tholos, and tall obelisks.
  - `SunsetShafts` (`shaders/env_world_shaft.gdshader`, `env_see_through`, 1 draw): five soft volumetric golden sunset shafts slanting down through the sunward front-right colonnade bays (`37°` to `117.6°`, staying clear of the viewer and sight line), fading out naturally as dusk deepens into night.
- **Seats:** `domestic` (`shell: "kit"`, `half_width: 10.5`, `front_z: -12.0`, `back_z: 9.0`, `height: 8.0`) lays out four rows of three (`12` seats, `seat_pitch: 1.25`, `row_pitch: 1.65`) rendered by `seats` (`model: "cushion"`, saffron-gold woven upholstery with ivory linen trim) resting squarely atop four low carved marble plinth benches (`y = -0.24` to `0.0 m`) with gilt-medallion end bolsters.
- **Systems:** `light` (warm dusk fill, `baked_fill: 0.45`, `spill: 0.65`, `accent` bound to `setting.braziers`), `domestic`, `seats`, `kit` (`cloud_sanctuary`, `cloud_sky_sea`, `bake_lamps: 8`, `flicker: 0.5`, `cove: 1.15`, `cove_floor: 0.35`, `water_motion: 0.8`), `sky` (`facing: 240`, `glow_bearing: 288`, `dusk: 0.18`, `afterglow: 0.90`, `evening_star: 1.0`, `milky_way: 0.75`, `milky_detail: 1.0`, `meteors: 8.0`), `acoustics` (`outdoors`, `rt60: 0.58`), `sound_bed` (`cloud_wind`, `fire` placed `around` at `radius: 6.5` following `setting.braziers`, and `temple_bell` at `[0.0, 3.5, -10.5]`).
- **Settings:**
  - `sky_mood` (*Sky mood*): `sunset_to_stars` (*Sunset to stars*, default — follows the film-time dusk track `0.18 -> 0.52 -> 0.92` and rising Milky Way), `golden_dusk` (*Eternal golden dusk* — holds `sky.dusk` at `0.18`), `starlight_clouds` (*Starlight clouds* — holds `sky.dusk` at `0.92`).
  - `braziers` (*Bronze braziers*): `lit` (*Lit flames*, default), `embers` (*Low embers*), `off` (*Off*) — controls brazier flames, ember crowns, and fire crackle sound bed smoothly.
  - `god_rays` (*Sunset shafts*): `soft` (*Soft shafts*, default), `off` (*Off*) — toggles the slanting golden sunset shafts.
- **Cues & timeline:** `arrive` rings the temple bell once on entering `preshow` and brings house lamps to `1.0` over `3.0 s`; `ritual` (when `ritual` is enabled) chimes the temple bell at `starting` and dims house lamps to `0.22` over `6.0 s` (`dim_direct` dims over `3.0 s` without the chime); `film_dim` holds `0.22` during `playing` and `credits`; `end_ritual` chimes the bell and lifts lamps back to `1.0` over `4.0 s`. Timeline tracks carry `sky.dusk` from `0.18` at `0 min` through `0.52` at `8 min` to `0.92` at `22 min` while `sky.hour` advances gently across the evening.
- **Comfort:** every brazier flame frequency is strictly under `3 Hz` (`0.37`–`2.07 Hz`) and stops cleanly when `Flickering lights` is off; `Motion` scales cloud drift, water ripples, and shaft wisps (`0` freezes them cleanly); every light and mood fade is smooth (`>= 1.5 s`); the sight line to the cinema screen is 100 % clear from all twelve seats; every kit physics box keeps `>= 0.5 m` clearance from `(0, 0, 0)`.

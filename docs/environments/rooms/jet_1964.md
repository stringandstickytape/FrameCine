# Jet 1964

Part of the environments engine: [`docs/environments.md`](../../environments.md) has the design and says where each part is.

`app/environments/jet_1964.env.json` (Journeys, built by **Gemini 4 Neon Extra High**), tested by
`app/tests/test_room_jet_1964.gd`. A glamorous 1964 mid-century transpolar first-class jetliner cabin crossing the
Arctic at polar night. Twelve burnt-orange woven-upholstery first-class recliners with sculpted sandstone-taupe shells,
ivory linen headrest antimacassars, flip-up laminate tray tables and chunky charcoal armrests fitted with dual-hole
pneumatic air-tube headset jacks sit beneath a ceiling-mounted 16 mm twin-reel movie projector aimed at a pull-down
bulkhead screen. Twelve small high oval fuselage windows look out onto a 1960s jetport terminal and canted glass
control tower at departure and arrival, and in cruise onto a vast moonlit Arctic cloud sea below and shimmering
emerald-and-violet Aurora Borealis blooming outside the starboard windows whenever the film falls quiet. The crossing is
as long as the film (`docs/environments/passing.md`).

## The crossing's evening

- **Tracks** (by `film.progress`): `sky.dusk` from 0.85 to 1.0 at 0.22 (smoothed 0.5 s); `sky.hour` from 21.5 to 23.8
  over the film (1 s); `airway.mist` rising from 0.24 at the gate to 0.44 in high-altitude cruise and easing back to
  0.24 on approach (0.5 s). The timeline has an 8 s start and 6 minutes of credits.
- **Cues:**
  - `dim` when `starting`: house lamps to 0.08, galley door glow to 0.10, window glow to 0.15 over 5 s, releasing
    `sky.aurora` over 6 s (hat-rack and aisle mood strips stay softly glowing via `cove_floor` 0.45).
  - `departure` when `starting` (requires the pre-show ritual setting): sounds the two-tone cabin seatbelt chime
    (`chime.play`), waits 1.2 s, and switches on the ceiling 16 mm projector (`presentation.switch_on`).
  - `lights_up` in the pre-show and at the end: house lamps to 1.0, galley door glow and window glow released over 4 s,
    projector off.
  - `aurora_swells` when `film.quiet` stays above 0.85 for 2 s while `setting.aurora` is `quiet` (in `playing` or
    `credits`): swells `sky.aurora` to 1.0 over 8 s.
  - `aurora_settles` when `film.quiet` drops below 0.5 (in `playing`, `credits` or `paused`): releases `sky.aurora`
    back to its quiet baseline over 12 s.
  - `arrival_chime` once `journey.progress` passes 0.92 for 2 s (7200 s cooldown): sounds the descent cabin chime.
- **Room settings:**
  - `aurora` (`Aurora`: In quiet scenes, Always, Off; default In quiet scenes).
  - `picture` (`Film print`: 16 mm print, Clean picture; default 16 mm print — controls both the `worn_print` film look
    and the subtle overhead 16 mm projector purr).
  - `headset` (`Air-tube headset`: off/on; default off — routes the film's audio through tinny 1964 pneumatic air-tube
    cabin headphones via `speaker: "window"`).
  - `mood` (`Cabin mood`: Warm ivory, Polar blue, Off; default Warm ivory — tints the overhead hat-rack and aisle
    carpet guide strips).

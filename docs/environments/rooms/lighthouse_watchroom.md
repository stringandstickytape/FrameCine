# Lighthouse watchroom

Part of the environments engine: [`docs/environments.md`](../../environments.md) has the design and says where each part is.

`app/environments/lighthouse_watchroom.env.json` (category `retreat`, author `Gemini 4 Neon Extra High`). *A circular whitewashed-stone and cast-iron lighthouse watchroom perched high on a stormy Atlantic headland, crowned by a slowly turning brass Fresnel lens sweeping golden beams across the sea.*

- **The circular lantern tower** (`lighthouse_tower`, `scripts/env/systems/kit/lighthouse_watchroom.gd`): a 16-bay circular coastal lighthouse watchroom (`r = 5.55 m`, centred at `(0.0, 0.0, 0.10)`) perched 26 m above a stormy North Atlantic headland. Weathered oak deck planks radiate around a circular aft spiral-stair hatch (`(0.0, 0.0, 3.75)`, `r = 0.92 m`), ringed by a riveted dark-iron perimeter deck flange and anchored ahead of the seating by an ornate inlaid burnished-brass and dark-slate 8-point nautical compass rose (`r = 2.50 m`).
- **Parapet drum, panoramic diamond glazing & ribbed copper dome:** a waist-height (`y = 0.0` to `1.05 m`) whitewashed lime-stone and riveted cast-iron parapet alternates sixteen recessed heritage-crimson cast-iron dado panels with dark-navy bolted stiles, gleaming brass rivets, recessed whitewashed stone reveals washed by per-bay indirect oil-amber sill coves (`PieceCove`), and a polished oak and brass coping rail. Above the sill, sixteen dark-iron lantern standards (`y = 1.05` to `5.40 m`) carry two brass transom rings, three symmetrical tiers of iconic criss-crossing helical diamond brass astragal mullions, and single-surface curved storm glazing (`PieceGlass`) backed by sixteen rain-streaked `weather.panes`. Overhead, a moulded bronze cornice ring with a seamless golden uplight cove (`PieceCove`) rises into a 32-segment patinated dark-copper conical lantern dome (`y = 5.40` to `7.62 m`) ribbed by sixteen converging brass rafters meeting a central cupola throat and louvered brass ventilator ball. Outside the glazing, sixteen whitewashed granite corbels carry a riveted iron exterior catwalk gallery (`r = 6.95 m`) with a three-rail storm balustrade (`y = 1.22 m`) and spherical brass finials visible all around the room.
- **Cinema screen mount & watchroom craft details:** the `4.10 × 2.31 m` cinema screen (`center: [0.0, 2.45, -4.55]`) hangs cleanly on twin brushed-brass and cast-iron nautical stanchions bolted to the oak deck and braced to the bronze cornice, leaving sweeping storm-glass views of the Atlantic night on both sides and across the upper lantern arc. Aft of the seating, a circular cast-iron spiral staircase with fourteen brass-nosed treads descends into a warm-lit whitewashed stairwell enclosed by a curved brass safety handrail loop; directly behind it stands a carved mahogany instrument pier crowned by a ship's watch bell and a live working brass ship's chronometer clock (`PieceClock`) flanked by gimbal oil storm lanterns. Along the port parapet sit a heavy oak watchkeeper's chart desk (unrolled parchment sea chart with mahogany scroll rollers, brass sextant, parallel ruler, leatherbound logbook, glowing gimbal storm lantern), a parapet-braced brass speaking tube, and a port-forward gleaming brass refractor telescope on a mahogany tripod aimed seaward. Along the starboard parapet stand an oak maritime signal locker with folded storm blanket and lantern, a stacked coil of braided hemp hawser rope, and a mahogany barometer plaque anchored to the starboard glazing standard and oak sill (backlit brass aneroid dial via `PieceWindows` beside a glazed Fortin mercury tube). Four gimbal brass oil storm lanterns (`PieceLamps`) warm the watchroom interior.
- **Overhead rotating Fresnel optical drum & stormy Atlantic outside** (`lighthouse_lens`, `LighthouseHall` in `scripts/env/systems/kit/lighthouse_watchroom.gd`):
  - `LighthouseLensRig` (centred overhead at `(0.0, 4.62, 0.10)`, cleanly clear of every screen sight line): a sculpted first-order brass-and-crystal concentric Fresnel dioptric drum (`LighthouseLensBrass` + `LighthouseLensCrystal`, opaque 2 draws) with compression rings, clockwork rotation gear crown, meridian ribs, stepped catadioptric crystal prism rings, warm glowing ivory mantle core, and twin opposite bullseye lenses projecting two outward-flaring golden-ivory volumetric light cones (`LighthouseBeams`, `shaders/env_world_shaft.gdshader`, `env_see_through`, 1 draw, pitched `-1.4°` so they sweep above the cinema screen out across the sea mist without blinding the viewer or washing out the picture). Rotates serenely at `0.09 rad/s` (~5.16°/s, ~70 s per revolution), scaled by `Motion` and controlled live by `setting.beam`.
  - `AtlanticOcean` (`shaders/env_world_water.gdshader`, opaque 1 draw, `y = -24 m`, `r = 172 m`): dark indigo-slate Atlantic swells animated with `Motion`, white surf foam rings around the cliffs and sea stacks, and silver moonlight specular glints aligned with the sky Moon.
  - `HeadlandOutside` (`shaders/env_ice_outside.gdshader`, opaque 1 draw): tapered whitewashed-and-crimson exterior lighthouse drum descending into a craggy wet dark basalt cliff base, seven outlying basalt sea stacks and foaming reefs, and distant dark mainland coastal hills dotted with twenty warm harbour lights.
  - `ChannelBuoy` (`shaders/env_ice_outside.gdshader`, opaque 1 draw): bobbing red-iron high-focal-plane harbour channel bell buoy seaward at `(-22, -24, -38)` with a gently pulsing emerald navigation beacon (`0.28 Hz`, strictly below `3 Hz`).
- **Seats:** `domestic` (`shell: "kit"`, `half_width: 5.6`, `front_z: -5.5`, `back_z: 5.7`, `height: 5.4`, `vault_rise: 2.2`, `rows_front: 0`, `rows_back: 1`, `seats_per_row: 3`, `seat_pitch: 1.15`, `row_pitch: 1.55`) lays out two rows of three (`6` seats, zero centre aisle) rendered by `seats` (`model: "club_chair"`, weathered oxblood leather on walnut frames), guaranteeing a dead-centre head-on seat at `(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)` facing `-Z` and an odd seat count with a seat at `x = 0.0` in every row.
- **Systems:** `light` (cool Atlantic upper fill with warm oil-lamp bounce, `baked_fill: 0.30`, `spill: 0.65`), `domestic`, `seats`, `kit` (`lighthouse_tower`, `lighthouse_lens`, `bake_lamps: 8`, `cove` bound to `setting.beam`, `cove_floor: 0.38`), `sky` (`latitude: 50.2`, `facing: 290`, `moon_bearing: 322`, `moon_altitude: 16.5`, clouds and wind bound to `setting.storm`), `weather` (outside-only rain in 3 perimeter areas strictly clear of the `r = 5.55 m` glass drum, `1,100` particles, 16 curved rain-streaked `panes` with `pane_drift` bound to `setting.storm`), `acoustics` (`hull`, `rt60: 0.68`, `damping: 0.34`, `wet: 0.28`), and four `sound_bed`s (`ocean_surf` at `[0, -14, -18]`, `rain_glass` placed `around` following `weather.rain`, coastal `wind` placed `around` following `setting.storm`, and `foghorn` at `[0, -6, -34]` every `95 s` following `setting.foghorn`).
- **Settings:**
  - `beam` (*Fresnel beam*): `sweeping` (*Sweeping beam*, default — rotates the overhead brass Fresnel lens and twin volumetric beams at `0.09 rad/s`), `steady` (*Steady seaward* — holds the beam cleanly off-axis to starboard at `-34°` toward the moonlit horizon), `lantern` (*Warm dome glow* — hides the outward volumetric cones and warms the ribbed copper dome cove to `1.55`).
  - `storm` (*Coastal storm*): `gale` (*Atlantic gale*, default — heavy outside rain, `pane_drift: 1.35`, storm clouds and full coastal wind), `rain` (*Steady rain* — moderate rain, softer drift and swell), `clear` (*Clear moonlit* — dry crystal-clear panes, bright silver Moon and starfield over a calmer sea).
  - `foghorn` (*Foghorn*): `regular` (*Regular*, default), `rare` (*Distant & rare*, level `0.42`), `off` (*Off*, silences both periodic and cue triggers).
- **Cues & timeline:** `arrive` sounds the headland foghorn once on `preshow` and brings house lamps to `1.0` over `3.0 s`; `ritual` (when `ritual` is enabled) sounds the foghorn at `starting` and dims house lamps to `0.20` over `6.0 s` (`dim_direct` dims over `3.0 s` without the horn); `film_dim` holds `0.20` through `playing` and `credits` while `cove_floor: 0.38` keeps the brass and stone silhouette softly legible; `end_ritual` sounds the foghorn and lifts house lamps back to `1.0` over `4.0 s`. A timeline track advances `sky.hour` slowly from `22.4` to `24.0` across a three-hour feature.
- **Comfort:** Fresnel lens rotation is ultra-gentle (`0.09 rad/s`, ~70 s per full turn) and freezes completely when `Motion` is `0` (along with Atlantic ocean swells, buoy bobbing, and rain/cloud drift); the distant channel buoy beacon pulses at `0.28 Hz` (`< 3 Hz`) and holds steady when `Flickering lights` is off; all mood and lamp fades smooth over `>= 1.0 s`; the sight line to the cinema screen is 100 % clear from all six seats; every kit physics box keeps `>= 0.5 m` clearance from `(0, 0, 0)`; all storm rain areas stay strictly outside the circular lantern glass.
